How to brew filter coffee

  1. Add powder. Put 3 to 4 tablespoons of coffee powder in the upper chamber and gently press the plunger disc on top.
  2. Pour hot water. Add near-boiling water, cover, and let it drip for 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Heat the milk. Bring full-fat milk to a boil while the decoction drips.
  4. Mix. Add about 2 tablespoons of decoction to a tumbler, then hot milk and sugar.
  5. Pull. Pour back and forth between tumbler and dabarah to cool and froth it.

Tips

  • Do not stir the powder while dripping, or the filter clogs.
  • Use fresh powder; it fades fast once opened.
  • Adjust the decoction to milk ratio to your taste.
