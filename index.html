Make South Indian filter coffee at home

Strong decoction, hot frothy milk, and a steel tumbler and dabarah. Here is everything you need to get that tiffin-shop kaapi in your own kitchen.

Start brewing

What you need

  • A two-chamber filter (steel or brass)
  • Coffee powder, ideally an 80:20 coffee and chicory blend
  • Fresh full-fat milk
  • Sugar to taste

Why it tastes different

The slow drip creates a thick decoction that holds up against a lot of milk. The "pulling" between tumbler and dabarah cools the coffee and adds froth.
